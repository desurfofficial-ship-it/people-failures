Famous Failures

Every legend has a chapter of rejection, bankruptcy, or spectacular flop. Here's proof that failure is often the first draft of success.

Explore the stories

Stories of Resilience

These people didn't succeed in spite of their failures — they succeeded because they refused to let failure define them.

01

Thomas Edison

Failed 1,000+ times inventing the light bulb

When asked about his failures, Edison famously said: "I have not failed. I've just found 10,000 ways that won't work." His persistence gave the world electric light.

Invention
02

Walt Disney

Fired for "lacking imagination"

A newspaper editor told young Walt he "lacked imagination and had no good ideas." He went on to build one of the most imaginative entertainment empires in history.

Entertainment
03

Oprah Winfrey

Fired from her first TV job

Oprah was fired from a Baltimore news station and told she was "unfit for television." She later became one of the most influential media figures of all time.

Media
04

Steve Jobs

Fired from Apple, the company he co-founded

In 1985, Jobs was ousted from Apple. He returned years later to lead its greatest comeback, launching the iMac, iPod, iPhone, and transforming the company.

Technology
05

J.K. Rowling

Rejected by 12 publishers

Harry Potter was turned down by a dozen publishers. Rowling was a single mother living on benefits. The series went on to sell over 500 million copies worldwide.

Literature
06

Michael Jordan

Cut from his high school basketball team

Jordan was cut from the varsity team as a sophomore. He used the rejection as fuel and became widely regarded as the greatest basketball player of all time.

Sports
07

Colonel Sanders

His chicken recipe was rejected 1,009 times

Harland Sanders pitched his fried chicken recipe to over a thousand restaurants before finding a partner. KFC became a global franchise when he was in his 60s.

Business
08

Albert Einstein

Labeled "mentally slow" as a child

Einstein didn't speak fluently until age 9 and was considered a poor student by some teachers. He later revolutionized physics with the theory of relativity.

Science
09

Abraham Lincoln

Lost multiple elections and businesses

Lincoln failed in business, lost elections for legislature, Congress, and Senate, and suffered deep personal losses. He went on to become one of America's greatest presidents.

Politics
10

Vera Wang

Failed to make the Olympic figure skating team

Wang's dream of Olympic skating ended early. She later pivoted to fashion and became one of the most celebrated bridal designers in the world.

Fashion
11

Stephen King

Carrie was rejected 30 times; he threw it away

King tossed the manuscript of Carrie in the trash. His wife retrieved it. The novel launched one of the most successful writing careers in modern history.

Literature
12

Elon Musk

Near-bankruptcy of Tesla and SpaceX

In 2008, both Tesla and SpaceX were days from collapse. Musk put his last money into the companies. They went on to redefine electric cars and private spaceflight.

Technology
